7. Bayes Theorem 

90% students who pass have high attendance (>80%)

40% students who fail have high attendance

60% of all students have high attendance

Probability that a student passed the exam given that they had high attendance

Formula:

P(Pass High Attendance ) = P(High Attendance Pass x P(Pass) / P(High Attendance)
